Bioinformatics

This lecture series offers an accessible introduction to bioinformatics with hands-on coding examples using BioPython. Starting from basic sequence handling and digital representations of transcription and translation, the series progresses to protein structural analysis, visualization, and protein-ligand interactions. Later sessions explore predictive modeling using machine learning and real-world applications such as lignad-based virtual screening in drug discovery.

  • Bioinformatics Session 1: A FASTA File, Sequence Object, and Sequence Extraction

    This YouTube video provides a taste of bioinformatics by introducing the FASTA file and simple operations with the file.

  • Bioinformatics Session 2: Digital Analogues of Nucleic Acid Processes: Transcription and Translation

    This YouTube session discusses the chemical structure of nucleic acids (DNA and RNA) and the digital analogs of nucleic acid processes: transcription and translation.

  • Bioinformatics Session 3: Protein

    This YouTube session discusses protein briefly and shows where and how to get protein information using BioPython.

  • Bioinformatics Session 4: Accessing Parts of Proteins

    This YouTube session shows how to access different parts of protein using BioPython.

  • Bioinformatics Session 5: Accessing Further Details of Proteins

    This YouTube session explores how to obtain further details of proteins such as theta, phi, and psi angles of a strand.

  • Bioinformatics Session 6: Visualizing Protein

    This YouTube session will focus on protein visualization. Just as we previously visualized small molecules, we will now learn how to represent protein structures in a meaningful and insightful way.

  • Bioinformatics Session 7 (Sample): Protein-Ligand Binding

    This YouTube session discusses how a protein works in a cell after a small ligand binds to it. It also shows how to visualize proteins, ligands, and protein-ligand complexes.

  • Bioinformatics Session 8 (Sample): Protein-Ligand Binding Affinity Model

    This YouTube session will discuss a machine learning-based protein- ligand binding affinity prediction model that utilizes the RDKitGridFeaturizer.

  • Bioinformatics Session 9 (Sample): Protein-Ligand Binding Affinity Model (2)

    This YouTube session discusses the Protein-Ligand Binding Affinity Model using Circular Fingerprint and Contact Circular Fingerprint.

  • Bioinformatics Session 10: Ligand-Based Virtual Screening & Compound Filter

    This YouTube session discusses a real-world application of the protein-ligand binding in drug discovery and development: The Ligand-based virtual screening and compound filter.
